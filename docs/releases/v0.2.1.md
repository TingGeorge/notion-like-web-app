## 改進

- **裁切圖片的控制點更自然**：原本八個白色方塊太大、太顯眼，改成跟 iPhone「照片」一樣的細線樣式：四個角是 L 形標記、四邊中間是短線，都畫在裁切框外側；圖片周圍改成深色底，拖曳時會出現三等分格線。可以觸碰、拖曳的範圍比以前更大，手指更好按。

## Improved

- **Crop handles look natural**: the eight large white squares are now thin marks as in Photos: an L at each corner and a short bar in the middle of each side, just outside the frame, on a dark surround, with rule-of-thirds lines while dragging. The touch targets are larger than before.
